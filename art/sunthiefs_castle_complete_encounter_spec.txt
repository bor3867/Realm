0. COMBAT CONTRACT — EXACT UNITS, DANGER, FAIRNESS, AND PERFORMANCE
=============================================================================

Reference solo player (VERIFY AGAINST ACTUAL GAME): 900 HP, 6.0 tiles/sec unhindered movement, 2,500 theoretical maximum single-target DPS, approximately 1,400-1,900 DPS in a real movement-heavy battle. If actual player HP, speed, defense, or DPS differs, normalize numbers BEFORE playtest. Damage specified below is EFFECTIVE HP loss after ordinary defense, not necessarily the projectile's raw damage parameter.

Coordinates: 1 tile = 16 native world pixels. All x/y arena coordinates below use tile centers from top-left origin. Angles in degrees; 0 = east, 90 = south, increasing clockwise. Every radial 'N-shot ring' has evenly spaced shots at alpha + 360*k/N unless another arrangement is given. Every 'fan N, angle A' includes both endpoints, centered on a snapshot of player bearing at lock time. No homing unless explicitly specified. Shot speeds are in tiles/second. Travel stops at given range, collision, or wall. Projectile image sizes are IN NATIVE PIXELS: generally 2x2 rune, 2x4 lance, 3x3 cinder, 3x5 axe crescent, 3x6 sun spear. Keep hitboxes at or slightly INSIDE the visible solid sprite; ignore decorative glow. Draw at integer pixel positions/nearest-neighbor where necessary. Boss sprite center, feet and attack origin must remain stable when rotating.

Bosses cannot hit player on the same frame as an unannounced telegraph; standard telegraphs >=0.70 seconds, lethal directional charges/beams >=1.05 seconds, major arena transformations >=1.40 seconds. If actual player movement is slower than 6 tiles/sec, lengthen warning/gaps. Allow >=0.30 tile clearance between PLAYER collision circle and safe-lane projectile boundaries after accounting for both radii. Stop same named beam/puddle from reapplying damage faster than every 0.50 seconds; avoid duplicate damage from a primary melee hit and its decorative projectile at the same instant. Keep current invulnerability system unless it fails fairness checks.

Combat is high-risk, NOT random unavoidable: demanding weaving and snap decisions, no unavoidable corner pin, impossible overlaid walls, invisible origin, cheap spawn-on-player shot, or teleport collision. Do not add forced disorientation/controls reversal. Avoid a giant unavoidable shotgun at point-blank range. No attack starts during load, gate cutscene, or player spawn protection.

IMPORTANT: attack cooldown listed is measured from END of that attack's active window. One boss may execute a PRIMARY move while independent SUPPORT attacks are active, but no two incompatible primary moves may animate simultaneously. An attack on cooldown is replaced by another eligible move, never a blank idle. Secondary emitters continue to fire independently. Avoid overlong invulnerability and scripts that reset on fast damage. A phase crossing queues the transition at the next safe combo boundary (max 3.0 sec delay), prevents skipping the next phase, carries excess damage into the new phase up to 5% max HP, and rewards strong DPS with 0.9-sec visible stagger following every third completed combo if the player reached a 5%-max-HP damage threshold during it. Do not make stagger spam interrupt the danger before the combo finishes.

No silent warnings. Telegraph coding: red/orange dashed lane = physical/hammer; violet dotted circle = arcane; golden spoke = solar; white flash and 2-tone chime = imminent release; unique one-line boss speech only at phase transitions/signature attacks; important countdowns also use icons/patterns, not color alone. Display large boss HUD with phase title, dual health bars for twin encounter, cast progress for a major signature. Sound cues must be short, distinct and rate-limited.

ENDGAME PRESSURE STANDARD: in normal active fight, primary starts every 1.4-2.5 sec where compatible and independent emitters release shots ~0.4-1.0 sec. Roughly 70-90% of active combat time contains navigable projectiles, with intentional 0.6-1.4 sec relief after major sequences. Attack DENSITY is calibrated to player speed, not arbitrary shot spam. A player cannot win by standing still or circling the boundary in one direction; aimed, inward, offset, and anti-rotation attacks force path changes. Test no-hit expert route and reasonably survivable recovery from a mistake; do not turn first mistakes into automatic death.

Suggested scaling if game supports more than one player: HP(n)=baseHP * [1 + 0.60*(n-1)] for 1-6 participants; always cap targeted simultaneous attack sources based on arena solvability. Never scale projectile count linearly with participant count. Do not implement multiplayer or netcode merely for this request.

1. THE CASTLE — WORLD EVENT AND ROUTING
=========================================

CASTLE PREMISE: The realm's sky fractures. A solar eclipse blots out the sun; ground fissures erupt and an enormous black-gold castle emerges above a sea of lava. Survivors are transported inside the outer breach after an unmistakable event warning. This is not a random dungeon: fixed landmark topology with small local variations, so players can learn mastery, and never generic interchangeable square rooms.

REALM ERUPTION EVENT (12.0 seconds total):
 t=0.0 on-screen banner 'THE SUN HAS BEEN STOLEN.'; rumble, darkening sky; mark persistent event zone.
 t=3.0 show cracking tiles as NON-DAMAGING environmental VFX. Message: 'THE CASTLE RISES.'
 t=6.0 a massive open rift appears; alert 'THE BREACH OPENS IN 6'. Countdown visible to all in range/qualified realm players, according to existing realm event rule.
 t=12.0 eligible players receive screen fade (0.50s), teleport to entry foyer with 2.5 sec spawn protection; do not teleport dead, disconnected, or ineligible players. If your game already uses a portal entry rather than teleport, adapt this transition without breaking existing entrance rules.

CASTLE VISUAL LANGUAGE: native crisp pixel-art architecture, volcanic stone and violet banners, uneven black basalt with visible cracks, forge chains spanning over chasms, molten reflections, falling embers, brass ornaments, long shadowy arches. Lava animation must not overwhelm enemy shots. Fixed landmarks; minor optional alcove variants only.
